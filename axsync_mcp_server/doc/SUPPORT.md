# Support

Published by AXSync, www.axsync.com.

## How to get help

1. Email hunain@axsync.com, or use the Contact the Author option on the Odoo Apps Store page of this product.
2. Include: Odoo version, module version, Odoo.sh or self-hosted, the AI client you connect (name and version), what you expected, what happened, and the relevant lines of the Odoo server log or the audit log entry.

## What is covered

Defects in the delivered module and questions about how it behaves. Help with a specific AI client, a reverse proxy or a firewall in front of Odoo, and custom tools or policies for your business, are separate work.

## Reporting a defect

Describe the steps to reproduce. For a connection problem, send the HTTP status that the client reports and whether `curl -i` against the /mcp endpoint answers with 401 (reachable) or fails before that (network or proxy).
